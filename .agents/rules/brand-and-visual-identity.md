# Brand & Visual Identity Rules

## 1. Core Brand Voice & Positioning
- **Identity**: AUREV SOLUTIONS is an authentic, founder-led technology and engineering initiative.
- **Tagline**: *"Engineering Ideas Into Intelligent Solutions."*
- **Tone**: Sophisticated, understated, intellectually rigorous, honest, and forward-looking.
- **Forbidden Brand Depictions**:
  - Never depict AUREV as an incorporated mega-enterprise or multinational corporation.
  - Never invent physical campus photos, server racks, labs, or corporate skyscrapers.
  - Never employ cliché startup buzzwords that overpromise without evidence.

## 2. Design Tokens & Palette
- Primary Deep Navy: `#10243A` (structural foundations, dark accents, editorial headers)
- Corporate Blue Accent: `#2457D6` (actionable elements, focused highlights, badges)
- Warm White: `#FAFAF8` (dominant background surface, clean and breathing)
- Light Grey: `#F0F2F5` (secondary card backgrounds, section dividers, metadata chips)
- Charcoal: `#20252D` (primary typography, high readability)
- Muted Blue: `#8DA8CC` (subtitles, secondary borders, understated technical metadata)

## 3. Visual Representation & Imagery
- Brand visuals must be geometric, architectural, and structural (representing ideas being engineered into real systems).
- Visual Fallbacks: When authentic project screenshots are not yet provided, use beautifully crafted, minimal SVG diagrams, typography cards, or schematic wireframes rather than generic stock imagery.
- Team Portraits: Only display verified, real photos provided by team members. Never use AI-generated human portraits.
